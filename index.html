Chabot DECA Milestones & Action Items

Window

Milestone

Action items

Aug – Sep 2026

Chapter kickoff

Recruit members, elect/confirm officers, set chapter goals; announce ICDC as the year's capstone.

Sep – Oct 2026

Competitive event selection

Match members to one of: Individual Case Study, Team Case Study, or Prepared Business Presentation events. Confirm no member double-enters outside the one-event-plus-prepared rule.

Oct – Nov 2026

Fundraising + budget plan

Build a per-member cost estimate (registration $160 + ~4 nights hotel at $335/night split per room + travel + meals); launch fundraising/sponsorship outreach; request institutional travel funds.

Nov – Dec 2026

Skill-building begins

Start regular practice: case study drills, roleplay practice, exam content review by instructional area.

Jan 2027

Mock competitions + qualifiers

Run internal mock case studies and presentation run-throughs; if your chapter has a state/regional qualifier, confirm those dates independently (they are not set by DECA Inc. and vary by association).

Jan – early Feb 2027

Travel logistics

Book flights/ground transport to Austin; confirm chaperone-to-student ratios; collect emergency contact + medical forms.

By Feb 15, 2027

Lock roster + pay dues

Hard deadline — submit final ICDC roster and pay Collegiate DECA dues in full (see official deadlines table).

Feb – early Mar 2027

Draft prepared-event materials

Finish written entries for any Prepared Business Presentation competitors well before the Mar 11–22 submission window.

By Mar 9, 2027

Register + book housing

Submit conference registration and hotel block reservations (see official deadlines table).

Mar 2027

Final prep push

Timed practice exams for Individual Case Study competitors (tested Sat Apr 10); final roleplay rehearsals for Team Case Study/Prepared Business Presentation competitors.

Late Mar 2027

Pack + logistics briefing

Confirm dress code compliance (business professional), distribute itinerary, brief chaperones and competitors on ICDC Delegate Practices & Procedures.

Apr 8, 2027

Travel day

Arrive Austin; hotel check-in opens at JW Marriott Austin.

Apr 9–12, 2027

ICDC

Compete — see official conference schedule above.

Late Apr 2027

Debrief + recognition

Chapter debrief meeting, thank sponsors/donors, recognize competitors, capture lessons learned for next year's officer team.
